# Northwind Plumbing: Customer FAQ (Sample, Fictional)

## Scheduling

We offer same-day service for calls received before 1:00 p.m., Monday through Saturday. Emergency service (active leaks, no water, sewage backup) is available 24/7 with an after-hours fee of $149.

You can reschedule up to 4 hours before your appointment window at no charge. Late cancellations are charged a $50 trip fee.

## Pricing

Our standard diagnostic visit is $89 and is waived if you approve the repair on the same visit. We give a flat-rate quote before any work begins. We never charge by the hour.

Water heater replacements start at $1,650 installed for a standard 40-gallon gas unit. Tankless installs start at $3,400 and require a site assessment.

## Warranty and Refunds

All labor is warrantied for 12 months. Parts carry the manufacturer's warranty, typically 1 to 6 years.

If you are not satisfied with a repair, call us within 30 days and we will return to fix it at no additional labor cost. Diagnostic fees are non-refundable once the technician has arrived on site.

## Service Area

We serve Concord, Walnut Creek, Pleasant Hill, Martinez, and Clayton. Calls outside the service area may be accepted for a $75 travel surcharge, subject to technician availability.
