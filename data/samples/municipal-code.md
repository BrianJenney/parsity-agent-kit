# City of Riverbend Municipal Code (Sample, Fictional)

## Chapter 8.12 Noise

### 8.12.010 Purpose

The purpose of this chapter is to protect residents from excessive and unnecessary noise while allowing reasonable use of property, tools, and equipment.

### 8.12.040 Leaf Blowers and Power Garden Equipment

Gas-powered leaf blowers may not be operated within 100 feet of a residence before 8:00 a.m. or after 6:00 p.m. on weekdays.

On Saturdays, operation is permitted from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m. On Sundays and city-recognized holidays, operation is permitted from 10:00 a.m. to 4:00 p.m. only.

Electric leaf blowers rated under 65 decibels at 50 feet are exempt from the hours above but remain subject to the general noise limits in Section 8.12.020.

### 8.12.060 Construction Noise

Construction activity requiring a building permit is limited to 7:00 a.m. to 7:00 p.m. Monday through Friday and 9:00 a.m. to 6:00 p.m. on Saturday. No construction noise is permitted on Sundays without a variance issued by the Building Official.

### 8.12.090 Penalties

A first violation results in a written warning. A second violation within 12 months is an infraction punishable by a fine of $100. A third or subsequent violation within 12 months is punishable by a fine of $250 per violation.

## Chapter 9.04 Short-Term Rentals

### 9.04.020 Permit Required

No person may operate a short-term rental (fewer than 30 consecutive nights) without a valid short-term rental permit issued by the Planning Department. Permits must be renewed annually.

### 9.04.030 Occupancy Limits

Maximum overnight occupancy is two persons per bedroom plus two additional persons, not to exceed ten persons total, regardless of the number of bedrooms.

### 9.04.050 Parking

Each short-term rental must provide at least one off-street parking space per two bedrooms. On-street parking may not be counted toward this requirement.

## Chapter 12.20 Sidewalk Vending

### 12.20.010 Permit Required

Sidewalk vendors must obtain a sidewalk vending permit and a city business license. Permits are valid for one year from the date of issuance.

### 12.20.030 Prohibited Locations

Sidewalk vending is prohibited within 25 feet of a fire hydrant, bus stop, or building entrance, and within 500 feet of a permitted farmers market during its operating hours.
